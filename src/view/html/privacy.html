Umgalelo Privacy Policy

Umgalelo respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

Information We Collect

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Profile Documents
  • Society Membership Information
  • Claims Information
  • Contribution History

Why We Collect Information

We collect personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Manage burial societies
  • Process claims
  • Track member contributions
  • Provide notifications and updates

Storage and Security

Umgalelo uses industry-standard security measures including password encryption, secure authentication, and protected cloud storage to safeguard personal information.

Your Rights

You may request access to your personal information and may request corrections to inaccurate information.

Contact Us

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact the Umgalelo administration team.
